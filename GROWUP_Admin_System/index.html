Costing with a business purpose

GROWUP Costing & Management Consultancy focuses on helping new, growing and established organizations build and improve the systems they use to understand costs. A useful costing system should reflect the way a business operates, explain its cost drivers and provide information management can act on.

Our focus

  • Practical costing systems designed around real operating processes.
  • Cost analysis and cost-control frameworks that make important cost movements visible.
  • Management-friendly reporting that supports planning and decision-making.
  • Implementation approaches that consider data quality, available resources and team workflows.

Tools support the system

Spreadsheets, dashboards and reports can be valuable parts of a costing solution. They are supporting tools, however—not a substitute for a well-designed costing methodology, appropriate cost allocation and reliable inputs.

Our commitment

We aim to understand the business before recommending a solution. Scope, deliverables and timelines should be agreed for each engagement based on the client's requirements. No client results, certifications or professional credentials are claimed on this website.
